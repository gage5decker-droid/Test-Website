Our Services

Complete Electrical
Solutions

Whether you're finishing a basement, wiring a new build, or need a single outlet installed — we bring the same precision and professionalism to every job, large or small.

i.
Residential Wiring & Repairs

Outlets, circuits, switches, and diagnostics for your home. Clean work, correct permits, and a crew that respects your space.

ii.
Panel Upgrades & Breaker Work

From replacing a single breaker to full service upgrades — done efficiently and correctly the first time.

iii.
Hot Tub & Camper Hookups

Dedicated 220V circuits, GFCI protection, and code-compliant outdoor wiring for hot tubs, spas, and RV plugs.

Fixtures, Fans & Lighting

Installation and replacement of light fixtures, ceiling fans, recessed lighting, and outdoor lighting — neat, fast, and finished right.
